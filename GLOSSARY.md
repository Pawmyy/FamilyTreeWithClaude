# Family Graph

A record of a family held as a graph of people and the things that connect them, so that any two people can be traced to each other.

## Language

### People and families

**Person**:
One human being, living or dead.
_Avoid_: Individual, member, node

**Family name**:
The surname a person carries. It belongs to the person, not to a couple, and may change during their life, most often by choice at marriage.
_Avoid_: Last name, clan

**Household**:
A couple and the children who belong to them, wherever they live. The couple need not be married, and one or both partners may be unknown.
_Avoid_: Union, marriage, family, house register

**Name record**:
The dated history of the full names a person has carried, each with the reason it changed. The latest entry is the name they go by.
_Avoid_: Alias list, name history

**Nickname**:
The informal name a person is called by, kept alongside their name record.

**Partner**:
A person who is one of the adults in a household.
_Avoid_: Spouse, husband, wife

**Child**:
A person who belongs to a household by birth, adoption, step-relationship or fostering.

**Parent**:
A partner in a household that a person descends from. Always worked out, never recorded directly.

### Relationships

**Descent**:
Belonging to a household by birth or adoption. Step and foster children are children of the household but do not descend from it.
_Avoid_: Bloodline, lineage

**Kinship**:
The named relationship between two people who share an ancestor by descent, such as "second cousin once removed".
_Avoid_: Relation, blood relation

**Family line**:
The chain of partners and descent that joins two people who have kinship.

**Closeness**:
How many steps apart two people are on the family tree, where parent to child is one step and partners are one step. Siblings are 2 apart, first cousins 4.
_Avoid_: Distance, relatedness, degree

**Link**:
Any recorded tie between two things in the graph that is not partnership or childhood, such as godparent or lived-in.
_Avoid_: Relationship, association

**Connection**:
Any chain joining two people, through links of any sort. In-laws and godparents are connections, not kinship.

### Finding and filtering

**Tag**:
A family name, a place or a year of birth, chosen in the search box to show only the people it fits. Everyone else fades.
_Avoid_: Filter, label, category

**Timeline**:
A chosen span of years, which may be a single year. People not alive at any time in it fade; people with no birth date are hidden, since the record cannot place them.

### The tree

**Line**:
The partner under whom a household's children are drawn in the tree: the one whose family name they carry.
_Avoid_: Bloodline, branch

**Founder**:
A person whose parents are not in the record. Each tree starts from one.
_Avoid_: Root, ancestor

**Marked copy**:
A second, faint appearance of a person in the tree, beside their partner. Their full appearance is under their own parents.
_Avoid_: Duplicate, alias

### Evidence

**Fact**:
One thing the record states about a person or a relationship: a name they carried, when they were born, who their parents were.

**Version**:
One account of a fact. Several versions of the same fact can stand side by side when people or documents disagree.
_Avoid_: Claim, revision, edit

**Accepted version**:
The one version of a fact that the graph, the tree, kinship and the map all use. Every fact has exactly one.
_Avoid_: Preferred, primary, the truth

**According to**:
Who gave a version: a person in the record, or anyone else by name.
_Avoid_: Author, editor

**Source**:
A piece of evidence a version rests on: a certificate, a register, a photograph, an interview, or a memory. One source can support many versions, and can carry one file, a picture or a PDF of the thing itself.
_Avoid_: Citation, reference, proof

**Edit log**:
The running list of every change made to the record, kept so that nothing removed is lost.
_Avoid_: History, versions

### Other things

**Photo**:
The single picture that represents a person or other thing in the graph.
_Avoid_: Avatar, image

**Family color**:
The color shared by everyone born with the same family name. It does not change when a person changes their name, and it can be chosen.

**Place**:
A location people can be linked to, by being born, living or dying there. A place may have a position on the map.

**Event**:
An occurrence people can be linked to.
